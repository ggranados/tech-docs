## Decision: 4 pages, grouped by mechanism (isolation vs. distributed consistency) and by paradigm-vs-specialized-workload

**Rationale**: Isolation levels and distributed consistency mechanisms are both extensions of the existing ACID/BASE/CAP trio but distinct enough concerns to warrant 2 pages, not 1 or 7. Data processing paradigms (batch/ETL/warehousing/lakes/MapReduce) are core architect knowledge; ML/AI/NLP/image-video/time-series are specialized workload types worth recognizing but not architecturally central to most systems this repo's audience designs — grouped and kept brief per the source spec's explicit lower-priority guidance.
